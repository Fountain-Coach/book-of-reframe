## Purpose

Illustrate the actual reading surface of two Reframe commands at their result moment: `/ground` declining to propose
a reading lens, and `/readings` comparing two readings of the same lines. Each command page requires its own
execution/result capture; a generic catalog screenshot is not acceptable proof.

## What the images contain

1. **Manuscript text** — James Joyce, *Ulysses*, episode 15 (Circe), two visible beat excerpts from lines
   20051–20216. This is a **new class of material for this register**: it is third-party authored text that is in
   the **public domain**, not publisher-authored material. *Ulysses* was first published in 1922 and its author died
   in 1941; the copyright term of life + 70 years expired on 1 January 2012 in the European Union. The text is
   assembled and served by Reframe's own corpus API, not copied from a third-party edition with its own editorial
   rights. No annotated, edited, or translated edition is reproduced.
2. **Reframe's own output** — the Copilot's replies, the reading questions, the uncertainty score and its lanes, and
   the reading-provenance strip. These are AI-generated text produced by the software under review and are published
   as evidence of what the software says, with that origin stated on the page.
3. **Application chrome** — window furniture, the chapter rail entry for episode 15, and the composer.

## What the images do not contain

- No personal data. No names, addresses, accounts, avatars, or contact details of any living person appear; the
  screenshots were reviewed for this before export.
- No credentials, tokens, keys, store paths with secrets, or deployment details.
- No raw FountainStore export, no private runtime source, no third-party licensed material.
- No unowned manuscript material. The publisher's own drafts do not appear in this scope at all.

## Ownership and direction

The publisher, Benedikt Eickhoff, directed that the screenshots from this drive may be published. The publication
policy requires a rights and scope review rather than automatic obfuscation, so the images are **full fidelity**: the
declared purpose is to show the command's actual reading surface, and an obfuscated capture would not be evidence of
it.

## Limits

- The public-domain determination above is for the EU term of protection. Where another jurisdiction's term applies,

- The AI-generated Copilot text is published as system output under the AI Act transparency row; it is labelled as
  Reframe's output on both command pages and in `SOURCE.md`, and it is not presented as human-authored editorial
  prose.
