Terms that must remain distinct

Book is not Bookshelf.

This Book

A reviewed, public narrative about Reframe's development. Its claims remain tied to dated records and checked evidence.

A manuscript

An authored source with its own identity and custody. Having a file, a PDF or a cover does not make it an admitted reading edition.

The Reframe Bookshelf

The writer's collection of explicitly admitted works and editions. Fountain Press composes their measured reading pages; this website does not grant admission.

A separately published reading, such as First Reading, is another kind of object—not a chapter of this Book.

The underlying method

Five acts, five different claims.

  1. SourceIdentify the material and its provenance.
  2. ScenarioName what must happen and what would count as evidence.
  3. ImplementBuild bounded behavior against that contract.
  4. AcceptWitness actual results, interfaces and persistence.
  5. PublishRelease only the separately reviewed public projection.
