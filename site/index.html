The simple model

A writer's path through Reframe.

Reframe separates the material, the interpretation, the action, and the proof. That separation is the point: a fluent answer is not automatically a trustworthy result.

  1. 01 · SourceA manuscript and its confirmed working context.
  2. 02 · ReadStructure, questions, and uncertainty become inspectable.
  3. 03 · ActA governed capability acts with its policy and preconditions.
  4. 04 · CheckThe result is visible, persisted, and independently evidenced.
