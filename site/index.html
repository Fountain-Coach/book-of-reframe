02 · The making

What changes—and what must not.

Publication is a sequence of accountable decisions. The source remains the source; every later projection has its own job.

  1. I

    The source

    Writing, original images, attribution and revision are identifiable before composition.

  2. II

    The edition

    An author admits a particular selection and order. A candidate or archived file does not become an admitted book by appearing in a folder.

  3. III

    The reading

    Fountain Press Linotype gives source-faithful material its measured leaves, contents and navigation. Reframe's private Bookshelf admission remains a separate writer action.

  4. IV

    The publication

    The publisher authorizes a public route and release; the deployed edition can then be independently visited and inspected.
