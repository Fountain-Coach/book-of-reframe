Csound AUv3 Instrument Boundary

The Book explains how a Csound Audio Unit fits into the Fountain Coach host story: Reframe remains the MIDI2 peer and mediator, the iPad hosts the AUv3, and Csound produces the audio.

Signature illustration

One boundary, four distinct authorities.

The picture explains the relationship. It does not turn a design projection into an acceptance or release claim.

The story

MIDI2 carries the intention; Csound makes the sound.

Reframe and the iPad host are separate instruments. MIDI-CI establishes the peer relationship. The AUv3 adapter owns the explicit host event mapping. Csound owns DSP. Reframe's Store and the independent witness answer whether the declared scenario actually completed.

Protocol

csound-auv3-instrument-boundary names the governed scenario and its lifecycle.

Host

reframe/audiounit.lifecycle is the instrument operation exposed at the boundary.

Evidence

A non-silent render, recording, Store receipt, AX result, and window identity must remain correlated.

Current catalog state

Designed, not released.

The Instruments catalog currently records this boundary as designed, not yet admitted or live-accepted. The Book therefore documents the semantic relationship and its proof obligations; it does not claim a released AUv3, AUM interoperability, certification, or universal hardware support.
