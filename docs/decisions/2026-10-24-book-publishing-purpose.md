# Book is about publishing — semantic reorientation

**Author direction (24 October 2026):** v22 reading-on links are obsolete Reframe development material. Determine what the domain is trying to tell, demonstrate and verify. The author's governing observation: book.fountain.coach is about **publishing**. Document and stage its replacement on the existing WLAN environment.

## Editorial proposition

**Publishing makes an authored work independently encounterable without losing the identity of its source.**

The Book domain introduces and witnesses publication of books. It is not a second Reframe Bookshelf, not a default importer, not the former Book of Reframe compilation, and not a generic EstatePublisher frontend. A book is an authored, source-bound edition with a readable experience, not a file, GUI screen or command count.

## Tell, demonstrate, verify

**Tell:** A book remains an identifiable work through original source custody, editorial selection/admission, composition and separately authorized publication.

**Demonstrate:** Encounter an already publicly published and readable book, First Reading — Solstice, at its actual original source-owned address. Follow a source-labelled entry, not a simulated book or an arbitrary mock cover.

**Verify:** Separate directly observed public route/readback facts, the edition's own contents and attribution declarations, documented software contracts, and facts not witnessed. Do not infer private Bookshelf admission, rights clearance, an App release, or specific typed EstatePublisher receipts from HTTP 200.
